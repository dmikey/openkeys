 HOW IT WORKS

Your piano is the controller.

Place this tablet on your music stand, allow microphone access, and play the highlighted note on your acoustic or digital piano. OpenKeys identifies the pitch and moves the music forward only when you are ready.

  • Flexible recognition allows for natural tuning and microphone variation.
  • For best results, play one note at a time and let it ring briefly.
  • Your audio never leaves this device.
